When Ashur-Muttabbil died, things were bad. With no food and no money, with her husband's family against her, Kunanniya didn't think that she would survive. "I'm going to die", she wrote to her sister. But then an opportunity arose, a small one, a single bolt of cloth to be traded. Then another, then another. She took each one, survived and prospered.

Today, it is not her husband that trades from Kanesh to Assur but her sons. It is one of her daughters that supervises the weavers, while the other is a priestess of Anna, in line to become the head priestess. And Kunnaniya herself presides over her precious records: the contracts, the records of trades, the accounts, the letters... and that final, precious letter from Ashur-Muttabil: "Cheer up your heart", he wrote.
