Raouwa Da Horouwe Ra Mha

"The bright-eyed one who is as light as a daisy". Dragons tend to be long-winded, but they do dote on their children.

used to have a hoard of gold just like any other self-respecting dragon. But lying on a pile of gold with nothing to do but wait for the next nitwit in a metal can to try to take it away can get very boring after a few years. So she started buying books: first one, then two, then a wagon-load, the as much as the eager merchants could drag up to her mountain home. Now her gold is nearly gone, but she doesn't care. In fact, she considers this her best decision since the time she decided not to eat that frightened girl that the fat bishop offered, but snacked instead on the fat bishop and his men-at-arms.

To be fair, her archival skills leave much to be desired. She once spent a month looking for a book and finally found it tucked away in a well-chewed helmet of a knight whose name she had long since forgotten.
