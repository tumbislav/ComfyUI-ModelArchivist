Raouwa Da Horouwe Ra Mha"The bright-eyed one who is as light as a daisy". Dragons tend to be long-winded, but they do dote on their children. used to have a hoard of gold, plundered from the kingdoms of the world, just like any other self-respecting dragon. But lying on a pile of gold for centuries, with nothing to do but wait for the next thick-skulled human in a metal can to try to take it away from you can get very boring. So she started buying books: first one, then two, then a wagon-load. As much, in fact, as the eager merchants could deliver to her mountain home. Now her gold is nearly gone, but she doesn't mind. In fact, she considers this her best decision since the time she decided not to eat that frightened girl that the villagers brought, but instead snacked on the fat priest and the men-at-arms who had tied the girl to a tree.

To be fair, her archival skills are lacking: she once spent a month hunting for a book. She finally found it tucked away in a well-chewed helmet of a foolish knight whose name she had long since forgotten.
